# 码上 · 隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 9 月 25 日

码上是一款在浏览器中生成和识别二维码的扩展。二维码内容和图片在设备上处理；开发者不设账号、分析服务或用于接收这些内容的服务器。

## 使用的信息

- 打开扩展时，扩展读取当前标签页网址，用于预填二维码内容。您也可以自行输入网址或文本。
- 只有您主动上传、粘贴或右键选择图片时，扩展才读取该图片并在本机识别二维码。识别出的内容只在界面中显示。
- 通过右键菜单发起操作时，扩展会在浏览器的临时会话存储中短暂保存图片网址或页面网址，用于将操作传递给弹窗。弹窗读取后即删除；浏览器会话结束时也会清除。

## 网站访问与网络请求

识别网页图片时，扩展会先请求您授权访问图片来源网站，然后从该网站重新获取图片。该请求可能按浏览器设置携带该网站的 Cookie；网站会像处理普通图片请求一样接收请求信息。扩展不会把图片或二维码内容发送给开发者或其他识别服务。您可以拒绝授权，改为上传或粘贴图片；已授权的网站访问权限可在 Chrome 扩展设置中撤销。

## 保存、共享与控制

扩展不保存二维码或识别历史，不出售或共享您的内容。您主动复制、下载或打开识别结果时，内容会交由浏览器、系统剪贴板、下载目录或目标网站处理。关闭弹窗会清除其中的临时内容。

## 政策更新与联系

如数据处理方式发生变化，本页会更新。问题和反馈可通过 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/maxhis/qr-blink-privacy/issues) 提交。

# QR Blink Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

QR Blink creates and reads QR codes in your browser. QR content and images are processed on your device. The developer does not operate an account system, analytics service, or server that receives this content.

## Information used

- When you open the extension, it reads the active tab URL to prefill the QR content. You can also enter your own URL or text.
- The extension reads an image only when you upload, paste, or select it from an image context menu. QR decoding runs locally, and results appear in the extension.
- For a context-menu action, the image or page URL is briefly kept in browser session storage to pass it to the popup. It is removed after the popup reads it and is also cleared when the browser session ends.

## Website access and network requests

To read a web image, the extension asks for access to the image's source site and fetches the image from that site again. Depending on browser settings, the request may include that site's cookies, and the site receives normal request information. The extension does not send images or QR content to the developer or a third-party decoding service. You can deny access and upload or paste the image instead. You can revoke granted site access in Chrome's extension settings.

## Storage, sharing, and control

The extension does not keep a QR or scan history, or sell or share your content. If you choose to copy, download, or open a result, your browser, system clipboard, downloads folder, or the destination website handles that content. Closing the popup clears its temporary content.

## Updates and contact

This page will be updated if data practices change. For questions or feedback, use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/maxhis/qr-blink-privacy/issues).
